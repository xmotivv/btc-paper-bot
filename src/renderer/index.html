Switch on as many strategies as you like. They trade the same paper account side by side, each with its own settings, positions and results, so you can see which ones earn their keep.

Connect a Polymarket US account to get its live order book, see its real balance, and let chosen strategies place real orders. Connecting alone only gives you live data for paper trading. Nothing trades with real money until you also mark a strategy Real money on the Strategies tab and arm live trading here, and it is always switched off again when the app restarts.

Each open position gets its own message in the channel, updated every few seconds with its unrealised P&L, and a Close position button.
